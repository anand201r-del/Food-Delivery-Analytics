        About this report

        Analytics layer for a food delivery platform in the style of Swiggy or Zomato. I designed the MySQL database, generated 1,00,000 historical orders (plus a new day of orders every night, using that day's real rainfall) across 49 cities in all 28 states plus Delhi, Chandigarh, Jammu & Kashmir and Puducherry, wrote the business logic inside the database, and answered business questions with SQL. Every number in this report comes from that database.

        What I built

        • Schema: 12 normalised tables (3NF) with primary and foreign keys, CHECK, UNIQUE and ENUM rules.
        • Data: Python generator calibrated to public figures (average order ~₹430, UPI-first payments, metros ~70% of orders) with regional food, opening hours, lunch and dinner peaks, regional festivals, region-specific monsoons, IPL evenings, and loyal and one-time customers.
        • Transactions: placing an order validates the cart, locks stock, applies a coupon, records payment and assigns the nearest free rider in one step, or rolls everything back.
        • Triggers: status audit log, delivery timestamp, rider release, live restaurant rating, out-of-stock flag.
        • Performance: 8 indexes verified with EXPLAIN; restaurant leaderboard 0.891 s → 0.156 s.
        • Reporting: 7 SQL views feed this report and Power BI.

        How to use this report

        1. Pick a page from the dark menu on the left (Overview, Customers, Delivery and more).
        2. Filter with the boxes at the top: choose a State (and, if you like, one of its cities) and a From / to month. Every chart updates at once.
        3. Compare two states or two cities: choose one first, then pick the other in "Compare".
        4. Hover or tap any bar, line or cell to see the exact number. Click a cuisine bar to filter by it.
        5. Clear filters appears next to the filters once you change one. It takes you back to everything.
        6. Each chart has a View SQL button that shows the MySQL query behind it.

        Words used in this report

        Revenue
        Money from delivered orders after coupon discounts. Cancelled orders are not counted.
        Average order (AOV)
        Revenue divided by the number of delivered orders.
        Late delivery
        Arrived after the time promised in the app (30–45 minutes).
        Lakh / Crore
        Indian number words. 1 lakh = 1,00,000 (one hundred thousand). 1 crore = 100 lakh (ten million).
        Active / At risk / Churned
        Last order within 30 days / 31–90 days ago / more than 90 days ago.
        Cohort
        All customers who placed their first order in the same month. Tracking them shows how many come back.
        RFM
        Groups customers by how Recently, how Frequently and how Much they order.
        UT
        Union territory, such as Delhi, Chandigarh or Puducherry.
        COD
        Cash on delivery.
